About

Sarosh Ahmed

I'm a video editor based in Pakistan, currently editing at Cogent Labs. I take on full projects, not just short clips. My work spans direct-response VSLs, long-form content, short-form social, and service-based ads, all judged by the same standard: does it keep someone watching.

I hold a degree in Computer Science from the National College of Business Administration & Economics (NCBA&E). I cut in Premiere Pro and After Effects, and lately I've been going deep on AI-assisted production: scripting and generating footage with ChatGPT, Higgsfield, and Claude, then layering that into a traditional edit. Certified through the Video Editing Masterclass at Editing Skool.
